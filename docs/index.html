Copy Any Youtube Transcript in One Click

Tired of manually copying YouTube transcripts? YouTube Transcript Yoinker adds a “Yoink Transcript” button next to Subscribe. One click to auto-open, clean timestamps, and copy plain text to your clipboard.

Get addon View on github

Why use Yoinker?

Unlike online transcript downloaders, Yoinker does everything locally in your browser. No external server, no account, no tracking. Perfect for students, researchers, and creators who need clean transcripts for notes and summaries.

One-click copy → clipboard

Works on video pages auto-opens
Timestamp toggle with/without time
Lightweight < 20kb
